# USB Video Camera privacy policy

Public copy of the privacy policy of the USB Video Camera Android app, published with GitHub Pages: https://krixoko.github.io/usbvideocamera-privacy/

| File | Served at | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `index.html` | `/` | Privacy policy, English (main page) |
| `de.html` | `/de.html` | Privacy policy, German |
| `en.html` | `/en.html` | Redirect to `/`, kept for older links |
| `foreground-service-demo.html` | `/foreground-service-demo.html` | Demo video for the Play foreground service declaration |
| `404.html` | any unknown path | Points back at the pages above |

**These URLs are referenced from the Google Play Console** (privacy policy URL and the foreground service declaration). Renaming a file, the repository or the GitHub account breaks them silently, and the next Play review fails. Do not rename; keep `en.html` as a redirect.
