What this is

USB Video Camera shows the image of USB cameras, endoscopes, microscopes and UVC HDMI grabbers and records photos and videos. The processing takes place on your device. The app has no user account, no servers of its own and no watermark. The app uses an internet connection for advertising (see “Advertising”), for usage statistics with Google Analytics for Firebase (see “Usage statistics”) and for the optional local stream (see “Stream on Wi-Fi”).

Camera and microphone

The app reads the image from the connected USB camera. Android requires camera permission for this. If you enable audio, the app can use the device microphone, a USB microphone or the source selected by Android. Android requests microphone access. Recordings stay on your device by default. Sharing and optional streaming can make the selected content available to other recipients. Background recording, time-lapse and streaming use a foreground service with a visible notification.

Recordings and settings

The app stores photos and videos on your device in Android's shared media storage. Notes, camera names and camera profiles (resolution, image correction and camera values per camera model) are kept in the app's private storage. You can share or delete recordings in the app's gallery. With “record on motion” and loop recording, the app deletes unneeded segments automatically. Automatic Android backup is turned off for the app. “Clear storage” in Android or uninstalling removes the private app data; recordings in the media storage remain until you delete them.

Export and sharing

When you export to a folder of your choice or share with another app, you choose the destination yourself in the Android picker. A cloud service or app chosen there processes the files under its own rules; the app does not upload them itself.

Stream on Wi-Fi

You start the stream yourself for use on your local network. MJPEG uses unencrypted HTTP. Optional RTSP streaming for players such as VLC or OBS uses unencrypted RTSP/RTP. Each protocol supports up to three simultaneous clients. Clients need the current access link or key and, if set, the password. Recipients on the reachable network can receive and save the streamed preview. The stream is served directly from the device. Stop it when you no longer need it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads from Google's AdMob service. A banner can appear below the photo, video and time-lapse buttons, never on the live image, and an ad can appear as the first entry and at the bottom of the gallery. A full-screen ad can appear at a natural break, for example after you close the gallery, finish a recording or disconnect the camera, and an app-open ad when you return to the app after a while. Full-screen ads never appear during a recording, time-lapse or stream. If you choose to watch a short video to unlock the Pro features for 24 hours, Google shows a rewarded video ad. Where Google shows no ad, for example in Russia, the app shows its own AliExpress offers instead; a tap opens AliExpress through an affiliate link, and AliExpress then processes data under its own privacy policy. To load ads, the app connects to the internet using the INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID permissions. Depending on your choice, Google may process data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and usage information to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure their performance. See Google's privacy policy. The advertising SDK does not receive your camera images, recordings, sound or notes.

Consent: at startup and where required by law (EEA, Switzerland, United Kingdom), the app asks for your choice through Google's consent platform (UMP). Where consent is required, processing relies on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. For device access requiring consent in Germany, Section 25(1) TDDDG applies. You may withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Google UMP can contact Google to check consent status before you make a choice. Whether ad requests can be made and which types are allowed depends on region, your choices and the signals returned by Google. You can change or withdraw your choice later under “More options” in the app. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings at any time.

In applicable U.S. states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for advertising. In the app, open “More options” and select “Ad privacy settings”. Google provides the form and processes your choice.

Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the app is used and how ads and Pro perform, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. It records usage events such as app starts, sessions, screens, ad impressions, in-app purchases and whether a Pro feature was unlocked, together with an app instance ID, device model, Android version, language and approximate country derived from the IP address. It does not receive camera images, recordings, sound, notes or file names. Google may process the data in the United States; Google is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. The developer sees the results only as aggregated statistics in Firebase and AdMob. Event and user data retention depends on the Google Analytics setting. Aggregated standard reports may remain available beyond that period.

The app initially sets the analytics and advertising storage consent signals to denied. Google UMP updates the signals based on your choices. Without permission, Google’s advanced consent mode may still process limited measurement signals. Where consent is required, that processing relies on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it in “More options” for the future. Where the status check does not require consent or the app does not show a form for Russia, the app uses Analytics for its legitimate interest in improving the app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), where applicable. See Firebase privacy information.

Pro and the optional purchase

Time-lapse, the Wi-Fi stream and measuring are Pro features. You can unlock them for 24 hours by watching a short video ad, or buy Pro once. Where the purchase is available, Google Play processes the payment. The app uses purchase information from Google Play to verify or restore Pro. It stores the Pro status and the end of a 24-hour unlock on your device so they also work offline. See Google's privacy policy.

No transfer to the developer

The app processes recordings, settings and the camera diagnostic log locally. The log records technical events. You can view it in the app and choose to share it through Android. Selected recipients can receive the data you share. Google Play can show an optional review dialog. A rating or comment you submit there goes to Google Play. Review retention and removal options are available in your Play account.

Contact by email and this website

If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and the content. These details are used to handle your request and deleted afterwards unless statutory retention duties apply. The basis is handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or the legitimate interest in technical support (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The contact address uses Gmail; Google processes data to provide that email service. This website is hosted with GitHub Pages; GitHub processes technical connection data, in particular IP addresses, when you visit. It contains no analytics scripts, ads or external fonts of its own.

Your rights

Where the developer processes personal data about you, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to a data protection authority.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and the developer does not knowingly process data about them.

Google Play and Android

Google Play, Android and possibly your device manufacturer process data under their own privacy policies. This policy describes processing by USB Video Camera.
