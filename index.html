Privacy policy for USB Video Camera Last updated: 25 October 2026 Controller and contact Alex Ovcinnikov c/o IP-Management #6542 Ludwig-Erhard-Straße 18 20459 Hamburg Germany Email: kroxoko@gmail.com (also for privacy requests) What this is USB Video Camera shows the image of USB cameras, endoscopes, microscopes and UVC HDMI grabbers and records photos and videos. The processing takes place on your device. The app has no user account, no servers of its own, no watermark and no analytics or tracking services. The app uses an internet connection for advertising (see "Advertising") and for the optional local stream (see "Stream on Wi-Fi"). Camera and microphone The app reads the image of the connected USB camera. Android requires the "Camera" permission for this, even when the camera is connected over USB. Optionally the app records sound with your device's microphone for video; Android asks for microphone access for this. Image and sound are not sent to the developer or to third parties. If a recording, time-lapse or stream runs in the background, Android shows a notification of a visible foreground service. Recordings and settings The app stores photos and videos on your device in Android's shared media storage. Notes, camera names and camera profiles (resolution, image correction and camera values per camera model) are kept in the app's private storage. You can share or delete recordings in the app's gallery. With "record on motion" and loop recording, the app deletes unneeded segments automatically. Automatic Android backup is turned off for the app. "Clear storage" in Android or uninstalling removes the private app data; recordings in the media storage remain until you delete them. Export and sharing When you export to a folder of your choice or share with another app, you choose the destination yourself in the Android picker. A cloud service or app chosen there processes the files under its own rules; the app does not upload them itself. Stream on Wi-Fi The stream starts only through an action in the app. It is meant for your local network: an MJPEG stream for the browser and optionally an RTSP stream for programs such as VLC or OBS, with a changing access key, at most three simultaneous clients and an optional password. The stream uses unencrypted HTTP. Anyone who knows the link and is on the same network can see the image. The app does not send images to a provider or to the developer. Stop the stream when you no longer need it. Advertising (Google AdMob) The app shows ads from Google's AdMob service: a banner on the start screen without a connected camera and at the bottom of the gallery, and occasionally a full-screen ad, at most every three minutes and only after the gallery is closed. No ads appear on the live image or during a recording, time-lapse or stream. To load ads, the app connects to the internet; for this it holds the permissions INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and AD_ID. Depending on your choice, Google may process data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device and usage information to deliver ads, prevent fraud and measure their effect. Google is responsible for this under https://policies.google.com/privacy. Your camera images, recordings, sound and notes are never sent to Google. Consent: at startup and where required by law (EEA, United Kingdom), the app asks for your choice through Google's consent platform (UMP). Without consent, no ads are requested. You can change your choice later under "More options" in the app. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in the Android settings at any time. No transfer to the developer Apart from the advertising data processed by Google as described above, the app does not transmit any personal data to the developer or other companies. Contact by email and this website If you send an email, the developer receives your email address and the content. These details are used to handle your request and deleted afterwards unless statutory retention duties apply. The basis is handling your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) or the legitimate interest in technical support (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The contact address uses Gmail; Google processes data to provide that email service. This website is hosted with GitHub Pages; GitHub processes technical connection data, in particular IP addresses, when you visit. It contains no analytics scripts, ads or external fonts of its own. Your rights Where the developer processes personal data about you, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may also complain to a data protection authority. Google Play and Android Google Play, Android and possibly your device manufacturer process data under their own privacy policies.
